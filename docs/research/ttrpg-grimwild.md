## 1. Executive Summary

***Grimwild*** is a cinematic, narrative-first tabletop role-playing game that reimagines the tropes, archetypes, and adventures of traditional fantasy (such as *Dungeons & Dragons 5e*) through modern, fiction-first design philosophies. 

Built on the proprietary **Moxie system**, *Grimwild* eliminates the procedural crunch, rigid grid combat, and bloated stat blocks of traditional d20 games. Instead, it synthesizes the best narrative mechanics from *Blades in the Dark*, *Dungeon World* (PbtA), *Fate*, *Cortex Prime*, and *The Burning Wheel*.

After the original crowdfunding creator (Oddity Press / J.D. Maxwell) became inactive in early 2025, the game's active community took over custodial development under a Creative Commons (CC-BY 4.0) license. The definitive version in active use is ***Grimwild: Community Edition*** (curated by Chancey Scott of Groov Games), representing the consolidated, polished ruleset.

## 2. Core Engine: The Moxie System

The Moxie system resolves risky actions using a lightweight, fiction-forward dice pool mechanic designed to generate dramatic, multi-layered outcomes.

### 2.1 The Core Action Roll
- **Dice Type:** Players roll a pool of six-sided dice (**d6**).
- **Pool Construction:** Typically 1 to 3 d6 based on the relevant core stat, augmented by character abilities, gear **Vantage**, or situational advantages.
- **Resolution:** The player takes the **single highest die**:
  - **6 (Perfect):** Complete success with no negative consequences.
  - **4–5 (Messy):** Partial success — the goal is achieved, but at a cost, complication, or with incoming harm.
  - **1–3 (Grim):** Failure or dramatic setback — the situation worsens significantly.
  - **Multiple 6s (Critical):** Exceptional success granting heightened effect, a secondary narrative benefit, or a setup bonus for allies.

### 2.2 The Thorns Mechanic (GM Difficulty)
Rather than adjusting numerical Target Numbers (DCs) or subtracting dice from player pools, the Game Master introduces **Thorns**:
- **What they are:** Eight-sided dice (**d8**) added directly into the player's action pool.
- **How they function:** If any Thorn rolls a **7 or 8**, the outcome quality is "cut" down by one step:
  - *Perfect (6)* cut by a Thorn becomes *Messy*.
  - *Messy (4–5)* cut by a Thorn becomes *Grim*.
  - *Grim (1–3)* cut by a Thorn becomes a *Disaster*.
- **Design Intent:** The physical contrast of d8s rolling alongside d6s makes danger immediately readable at the table without any math. Because d6s cannot roll 7 or 8, the threat is visually distinct.

### 2.3 Diminishing Task Pools
Complex obstacles, dungeon tension, tracking, or large monsters use **Diminishing Pools**:
- A pool of dice (e.g., 4d6 or 6d6) represents an enduring challenge.
- When progress is made, the pool is rolled. Any dice showing **3 or less** are discarded.
- Once the pool reaches zero dice, the challenge is resolved or the fictional state decisively shifts.

### 2.4 Spark & Tangles (Meta-Currency)
- **Spark:** A heroic momentum resource. Players spend Spark to add bonus dice or exert narrative control.
- **Tangles:** Players gain Spark by voluntarily leaning into character flaws, triggering narrative complications ("Tangles"), or weathering Disasters.

## 3. Character Framework

*Grimwild* models characters using narrative descriptors rather than hundreds of pages of mechanical lists.

### 3.1 The Four Core Stats
Rated from **1 (Poor)** to **3 (Great)**:
| Stat | Physical / Mental | Governs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brawn** | Physical | Brute force, athletic power, resilience, menace, bodily toughness |
| **Agility** | Physical | Speed, reflexes, acrobatics, manual dexterity, ranged precision |
| **Wits** | Mental | Knowledge, deduction, awareness, tactical trickery, reading scenes |
| **Presence** | Mental | Charm, force of personality, willpower, command, reading people |

### 3.2 The 12 Reimagined Classes
*Grimwild* embraces the iconic fantasy archetypes from 5e/d20 fantasy, translating them into flexible, ability-driven playbooks:
1. **Barbarian** — Primal rage, indomitable resilience, reckless momentum.
2. **Bard** — Inspiration, manipulative banter, magical performance.
3. **Cleric** — Divine channel, spiritual touchstones, holy warding.
4. **Druid** — Shifting forms, wild spirits, environmental communion.
5. **Fighter** — Martial superiority, tactical leadership, combat stances.
6. **Monk** — Focused discipline, martial arts, inner balance.
7. **Paladin** — Sacred oaths, protective auras, zealous smiting.
8. **Ranger** — Wilderness tracking, beast companionship, skirmishing.
9. **Rogue** — Stealth, ambush, thievery, exploiting openings.
10. **Sorcerer** — Raw innate magic, chaotic surges, bloodline powers.
11. **Warlock** — Eldritch bargains, alien patrons, persistent hexes.
12. **Wizard** — Arcane scholarship, versatile preparation, ritual spellcraft.

### 3.3 Levels & Progression
- Progression spans **Levels 1 to 7**.
- Rather than calculating monster XP, advancement is driven by self-selected **character arcs**, narrative milestones, and session goals.

### 3.4 Damage & Health: Conditions over Hit Points
*Grimwild* does away with bloated bags of Hit Points:
- **Harm is represented by Conditions**:
  - **Bloodied:** Physical injury impacting physical rolls (Brawn/Agility).
  - **Rattled:** Psychological shock, terror, or exhaustion impacting mental rolls (Wits/Presence).
- **Dropped:** When harm stacks beyond thresholds, the character is dropped out of the scene (captured, unconscious, or at death's door depending on the stakes).

### 3.5 Free-Form Magic ("Touchstones")
Spells are not Vancian lists with daily slots. Spellcasters choose **Touchstones** (thematic spheres of magic such as *Flame*, *Shadow*, *Tides*, or *Memory*) combined with universal spell intents:
- *Attack* (offensive strikes)
- *Defend / Ward* (protective barriers)
- *Move* (teleportation, levitation, kinetic force)
- *Detect* (divination, sensory reach)
- *Alter* (transmutation, illusion, status disruption)

## 4. GM Framework & Adventure Running

The Game Master's toolkit in *Grimwild* is modeled on modern narrative engines (PbtA/FitD):

### 4.1 GM Moves & Suspense
- The GM does not roll to attack. When players roll a *Messy* or *Grim* result, the GM responds with a **GM Move**.
- **Story Moves:** Foreshadow danger, reveal an unwelcome truth, use up their resources, turn their move back on them.
- **Suspense:** A GM meta-currency pool accumulated as danger rises, spent to trigger devastating **Impact Moves** or escalate scenes.

### 4.2 Monster Design
Monsters are concise narrative prompts rather than math matrices:
- **Tier & Scale:** Threat level and narrative magnitude.
- **Task Pool / Durability:** How many diminishing dice are needed to overcome the threat.
- **Thorns:** Inherent danger rating adding d8 Thorns to player attacks.
- **Impact Moves:** Dramatic scripted interventions (e.g., *Shatter the Floorboards*, *Swallow Whole*, *Terrifying Gaze*).

### 4.3 The GM Crucible
A zero-prep procedural generator built into the system. The GM rolls on evocative keyword tables (Motives, Omens, Terrains, Twists) to assemble adventure locations, NPC attitudes, and sudden turns on the fly.

## 5. Direct Applications for *Gazetteer*

As an RPG scenario editor and module publisher, *Gazetteer* can directly support *Grimwild* adventures alongside *D&D 5e*, *Shadowdark*, and *Pathfinder 2e*:

| Gazetteer Entity | Grimwild Moxie Representation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Enemy / Boss** | Name, Threat Tier, **Thorns (d8)**, **Task Pool (e.g. 5d6)**, **Impact Moves**, Vulnerabilities |
| **Trap / Hazard** | Trigger, Detection/Disarm skill (Wits/Agility), **Thorns (1–2 d8)**, Consequence (*Bloodied*, *Rattled*, or *Structural Collapse*) |
| **NPC** | Role, Demeanor, Core Stat Highlights (Brawn/Agility/Wits/Presence), **Tangle Hook** (for player Spark generation) |
| **Area / Site** | Description, Sensory details, Connected locations, **Diminishing Tension Pool** |
| **Item / Treasure** | Name, Rarity, **Vantage Bonus** (+1d on specific action), Magical Touchstone synergy |
